# Sap Quest

An 8-bit trivia game about plant transport (roots, xylem, phloem, transpiration) for lower secondary students.

**Play:** https://vanoofiahkahi.github.io/sap-quest/

## Modes (the teacher chooses)
- **One screen** - everyone plays on the teacher's screen.
- **Device per team** - each team of 2 joins on one device with a 6-digit code.
- **Device per student** - every student joins and taps a team (2 per team).

## Features
- 2-15 teams, each with its own clock
- Pick a topic and points: higher points mean harder questions
- Wrong answers can be stolen by other teams (first to tap STEAL)
- 2 rounds, 50 questions, chiptune music and sound effects
- Teacher monitor: who is online, scores, answers and a live activity log

Devices connect peer-to-peer through PeerJS, with the teacher's browser acting as the game server. Keep the teacher's tab open during the game.
